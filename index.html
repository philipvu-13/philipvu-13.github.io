Philip Vu Health Tracker

A personal health tracking tool

This is a private tool built and used by one person, Philip Vu, to track his own fitness and nutrition data.

It reads health data from the Google Health API for a single account, the owner's own, and stores it locally on his own computer. It exists so that daily calorie, activity, sleep and weight figures can be viewed alongside a food log in one place.

What the tool reads

  • Activity and fitness data such as calories burned, active zone minutes and steps
  • Health metrics such as weight, body fat, resting heart rate and heart rate variability
  • Sleep duration and sleep stages
  • Basic profile information

Access is read only. The tool cannot create, change or delete any health data. It has no other users, no accounts, no sign up and no public interface.
